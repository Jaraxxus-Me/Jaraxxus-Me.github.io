About me

I am a Robotics Ph.D student at Robotics Institute, CMU, advised by Prof. Sebastian Scherer. I also collaborate closely with Prof. Tom Silver.

Prior to CMU, I obtained my B.ENG in Mechanical Engineering from Tongji University (TJU) with the highest honors.

My past (undergrad/master) research were about Generalizable Object Perception in Challenging Environments, details see Here.

Now (Ph.D. thesis), I work on Long-horizon Emboided Reasoning. Specifically, I'm excited about how robots can learn to achieve long-horizon physical tasks in a generalizable and smart way. Techniques that I am particularly interested in and actively working on include:

  • High-level Concept Discovery (via Hybrid Optimization, or Foundation Models)
  • Robotic Program Synthesis (via LLMs+Verifier, or Agentic Frameworks)
  • Deep Reinforcement Learning and Planning (for Shortcut Discovery, or Failure Recovery)

    • I am actively looking for 2027 spring research internships on agentic sim2real and/or long-horizon (loco-/dex-)manipulation. Feel free to reach out if I am a good fit for your team!
